Charlotte homeowners get billed for a “septic visit” that never finds the tank, never pumps, and never writes what was seen. Use this list before you book.

  • Ask if they will open the lid — not only walk the yard or poke with a rod.
  • Ask whether you are paying for a pump-out, a written inspection, or both on the same trip.
  • Ask what happens if the lid is buried and they have to locate or dig.
  • Ask for sludge level, baffles, and drain field in a written note you can keep.
  • Ask whether a repair or replacement needs a county on-site wastewater permit (Mecklenburg or the county you live in). Routine pumping usually does not.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website and run through this list.
